# Data retention

The onboarding team records decisions in the Lumen issue tracker. For data retention, assign an owner and document the acceptance criteria.

## Procedure

1. Review the linked issue and its affected services.
2. Ask the owner to confirm changes before applying them.
3. Record the outcome and follow-up date in the team notes.
